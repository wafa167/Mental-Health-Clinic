About Dr. Alexander Wright

With over 15 years of experience in psychiatric medicine, Dr. Wright founded MindBalance Clinic to provide personalized, compassionate mental health care in a comfortable private practice setting.

Trained at Johns Hopkins University, Dr. Wright believes in treating the whole person, not just symptoms. His approach combines evidence-based treatments with genuine human connection.

Professional Philosophy

"Mental health treatment should be collaborative, destigmatized, and tailored to each individual's unique needs and circumstances. My goal is to empower patients through understanding and treatment."
